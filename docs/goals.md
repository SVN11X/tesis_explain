## Vision

An open guide to the thesis "Navegación y exploración autónoma de interiores utilizando un robot móvil de tracción diferencial" that helps the author defend it and helps anyone learn its topics, and that keeps growing after the defense.

## Users

- The author, preparing the defense with the hard questions and the AI oral-defense practice.
- The thesis committee and professors, who check the site against the thesis.
- Students and the public who want to learn mobile robotics, including people who find it hard to keep their attention on long texts.

## Objectives

1. Prepare the defense: hard questions and oral practice with useful AI feedback.
2. Support the committee review: every figure and claim matches the thesis and is easy to check.
3. Teach the public: a student learns mobile robotics with the guide and its interactives.
4. Serve as a professional portfolio.

## Non-goals

- Explaining with a lot of text. Topics are explained in creative ways instead: interactives to manipulate, step-by-step diagrams, short challenges inside each topic, and a short summary with the detail folded. The full detail is kept, and it shows only when the visitor asks for it.

## Success criteria

- The author reaches the defense with the questions practiced and with useful correction.
- No figure on the site differs from the current version of the thesis.
- Everything can be read and used on a phone and with the keyboard alone.
- The oral-defense AI correction has measured agreement with human reviewers.
- Visitors learn the topics better, and the site holds the attention of people with attention difficulties.

## Priorities

1. Creative visual and interactive explanations over long text. The full detail stays available, only on demand.
2. The four objectives weigh the same. The owner gave no order between them.

## Constraints

- Free plans only: no paid services and no keys in the site. GitHub Pages and the Cloudflare free plan.
- Academic notice: the site says where the thesis stands (in its defense process on 2026-11-19) and does not anticipate the committee's conclusions.
- No visitor data: answers, audio and personal data are not stored, and there are no user accounts.
- Public URLs do not change: page file names and paths stay the same. The site is published with a GitHub Actions Pages workflow (owner decision of 2026-11-19).
- The site and its documentation are in Spanish. The standard's own files (this file, `DESIGN.md`, `AGENTS.md`, `.standard.yml`) are in English.
